# Collider-free preview recovery — 2026-10-08

Mac reproduced a blank inventory body after entering as Vanguard. The player log reported missing MeshCollider, followed by CollectionModelPreview.CreateContact dereferencing the absent Collider. The contact shadow used GameObject.CreatePrimitive(Quad), despite requiring only rendering. ProjectSettings enables stripEngineCode. Unity documents that CreatePrimitive adds a collider and requires its component types to survive stripping: https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/ScriptReference/GameObject.CreatePrimitive.html . The exact native linker output was not available here; missing component registration is established by the Mac log, not a locally reproduced IL2CPP build.

Both repositories now construct an owned four-vertex UV-mapped quad with explicit MeshFilter and MeshRenderer, with no primitive or collider allocation. Winding/normals preserve the upward-facing contact surface after rotation. Dispose releases the mesh, including stage-allocation failures. No link.xml preservation or engine-stripping settings change is needed for this visual-only surface.

Inventory wear, fashion/collection and reward model entry points all use RenderSafe. It cleans failed resources, preserves framing selection, logs the original exception once until recovery and retries at most every two unscaled seconds. Other page controls continue drawing with a placeholder. Strict Render and the light-state restoration tests remain unchanged; this is not exception swallowing in place of the root fix.

Audited CombatModel hero/equipment/fashion and ProceduralVisuals paths: no CreatePrimitive calls. Existing authored accessory colliders are only disabled, never required. Remaining primitive uses in GroundLootPickup, EnemyController health bars, SummonedCompanion and EnemyDeathDissolve are outside the preview/mannequin path; their collider handling is null-checked or destruction without dereference. Those unrelated world/FX paths are not changed in this hotfix.

Regression coverage: production preview lifecycle with primitive colliders absent; zero primitive allocations; owned quad UV/winding and mesh disposal; repeated render failure, throttled retry, single diagnostic and recovery. A compiled old-contact mutation reproduces the same CreateContact NullReferenceException with no Collider. Managed resource/GUI stand-ins do not establish GPU output or IL2CPP/device acceptance. Mac must rebuild the new SHA and retest opening/reopening inventory, class/gear previews, fashion trials and rewards. The iPad title enlargement remains present; main remains unmerged.
