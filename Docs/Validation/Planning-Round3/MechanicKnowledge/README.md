# Character mechanism knowledge

Base fce5efc4361614aed3eca070ed2574b7d7204a8e. Character-local variantKnowledge list/revision stores unlocked mechanism families; item mechanicVariant remains its selected A/B. Four shards learn a family once. Other copies acquire eligibility, never item level/rarity/reinforcement or an automatic B selection. Other characters and other mechanism families do not inherit knowledge.

HasVariant is the authority for service eligibility, preset capture/validation, progression goals and desktop/mobile workshop labels. Persistence normalizes each owned item's capability cache from knowledge; combat and item-only presentation continue reading that normalized item flag plus its selected variant. Applying a preset explicitly restores its recorded per-item variant and flag. Reforge/ascension preserve these existing fields.

Legacy revision zero unions only repaired, valid unlocked equipment from inventory/pending/recovery; equipped IDs, preset selections and discoveredMechanics do not prove knowledge. Load performs migration write before active profile/slot publication. Failure retains the original in-memory owner and disk. No shard refunds. Revision one preserves learned families even after item sale and filters illegal/cross-class entries.
